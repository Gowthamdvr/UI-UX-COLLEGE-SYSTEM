Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a transcript?

Transcripts can be requested through the student portal or by contacting the Registrar's Office directly.

Where is the admissions office?

The Admissions Office is located in the Main Building, Room 101. Visitor parking is available in Lot A.

How can I schedule a campus tour?

Campus tours can be scheduled online via the 'Visit' page or by calling the Welcome Center.
