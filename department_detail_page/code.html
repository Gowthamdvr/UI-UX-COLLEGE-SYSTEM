About the Department

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering was established to provide world-class education in computing. We focus on theoretical foundations as well as practical applications. Our curriculum is designed to keep pace with the rapidly evolving technology landscape, offering students opportunities to work on real-world projects, research initiatives, and industry collaborations.
